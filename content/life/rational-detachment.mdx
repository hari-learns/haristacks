There is this one skill I learned along the way of life which almost feels like a superpower. My Mom and my girlfriend has asked me how I do that when I perform it at times. The ability to move on from anything.

I am gonna coin it **Rational detachment**.

Rational because it makes sense and I am forcing it because it seems irrational for an emotional person. Detachment because we are letting it go. When something bad happens to us, we tend to hold on to that for a while which is perfectly humane but what if we could behave optimally here and make a decision to simply move on from it far easier than it usually takes?

Take this scenario, Lets say you had cooked a delicious meal, the one you love so much and would hesitate to share with anyone, It took a lot of effort. Now you get an important phone call, when you go on to attend that you accidentally forgot to simmer/do the thing that keeps the food safe. The call got your full attention and you come back to your food. Ruined.

You switch the gas off, try to get save it but Its gone. Now you are hungry , disappointed and in utter disbelief. You react audibly and curse yourself, takes a minute to actually process it .. for few of us.. takes years cuz just imagine that (completely valid).

Let’s say I walk into you that exact minute and tell you that, forget about this then. **snap. done. what next?** lets order or lets make a simple toast and get on with the day? We are not letting this thing ruin the entire day, we got work. And to be honest, call me crazy. we did lose nothing. Shit happens all the time bro

you are gonna slap me, I didn’t put in the effort for it, I don’t know about the pain you would endure at this time and I will take that slap but you have to listen to me now, Ily.

Its not like I didn’t care what you had cooked went to dust but I simply accepted the fact that thats not recoverable and looked to see whats next. I would feel bad trust me.

I called this ability of mine “F it we ball” move and later I realized this was a real thing and people called it proactivity.

* * *

To elaborate, psychologically let me tell you briefly about proactivity in detail, the first habit in the very famous “7 Habits of highly effective people”.

Stephen R Covey has mentioned in his book that there was this guy called Viktor Frankl a Jewish psychiatrist who was imprisoned in Nazi concentration camps. He lost almost his entire family there and He was tortured, humiliated, and forced to live in constant fear, never knowing whether he would survive or be sent to his death.

Before this, Frankl came from a psychology background that believed people are mostly shaped by their past. It suggested that childhood, environment, and suffering decide who a person becomes.

But in the camps, Frankl discovered something deeper.

The Nazis could control his surroundings. They could take away his clothes, comfort, family, freedom, and dignity. They could hurt his body. But they could not control his mind completely. He realized that even in the worst situation, there was one freedom no one could take from him: **the freedom to choose his response**.

He could choose whether suffering would destroy him completely, or whether he would give it meaning. In his mind, he imagined himself in the future, standing in a classroom and teaching students about what he had learned from his suffering. This helped him survive mentally. Frankl understood that humans are not just controlled by instinct, past experiences, or environment. We have self-awareness, imagination, conscience, and willpower. Because of these, we can step back, think, and choose how to respond.

Insane takeaway from this is that,

> _Between what happens to us and how we respond, there is a space. In that space, we have the power to choose. That choice is our freedom._

All you have to do is take a step back and see the position you are in again and play a third person game, what can you do right now that makes this situation better? Is it something we messed up so bad we cant recover? either way you know exactly what to do. Improvise or accept both leading to your peace and detachment from this scenario.

Its almost feels like a cheat code to me, I am not gonna say I don’t feel sadness. I do deeply and no matter how proactive I am i don’t think i can use this ability when Arsenal loses another champions league final, you know what I mean. I am not recovering from that nor accepting that. Sod off.

(I am an human.. after all)

Whenever something bad happens, someone curses at you, you made a blunder and instantly realize it, your brain gets stimulated. in that moment , become self aware and be proactive on how you could simply make that scenario better OR in some cases where you think its done, cant be recovered. You have to accept and move on.

It’s like you find your way out to breathe easy again. The skill takes time and experience to master but once you get going and handle all adversity and hardships trying to be proactive and not reactive. you will see the magic yourself.

in the future, we are gonna take the Ls, we gonna mess up the easiest things, we will make bad decisions. What I mean is that we will be perfectly humane until the end, I am just asking you to detach from all those that hold you back and just live.

Important thing, you cannot apply this to everything. No one can, Some bad stuff that happens to us are cruel and almost kill us. we are people who love things deeply , we love our pet the same way we love anyone. We invest in our business like Its our own children giving our everything to it. When cruel things happen to us unfortunately yet naturally , I think we should grieve rightfully and time be given to us as much as we want to get back but my dear, life is too short. nothing is permanent, including ourself.

There were a million years before us and a million would come after we had gone. No hardship should keep you long enough to waste this incredibly short yet wonderful life.

> We are nothing but a passing cloud to the mountains and oceans
